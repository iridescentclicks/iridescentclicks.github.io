Iridescent is an independent nature photography practice focused on wilderness, atmosphere and the changing language of light. I work slowly, looking for the fleeting details that make an image feel remembered rather than simply seen.

Available for editorial, travel, conservation and selected commissioned projects. Based between cities, always following the next interesting frame.
